## 1. How to read
- 100 tech companies from the H-1B transfer research. Grouped by priority, then roughly by transfer volume and fit.
- Stripe, Block and fintech are in the finance doc. The full 200 stays in the research report.
- **Company -N** = priority. -0 apply now. -1 apply this week. -2 opportunistic or has a caveat.
- **Comp cell:** `$TC = $base + ($stock, $bonus)`. US dollars, thousands, per year, one midpoint each.
  - Two columns: **SWE2** (mid) and **Sr SWE** (SWE3).
  - Stock = grant spread over 4 years at today's value. Bonus = target cash (Amazon, Oracle: sign-on spread or zero).
  - `~` = my estimate. `~~` = very wide (private stock or volatile). No mark = anchored to the research report.
  - Sr SWE is rough: explicit estimate for 32 companies, else SWE2 TC × 1.4 (public) or × 1.5 (startups).

## 3. Watch-outs
- **Microsoft, Adobe, LinkedIn:** PERM suspended Oct 8, 2026. H-1B works, green card stalls. Microsoft also offered voluntary exit to ~60K staff.
- **Walmart Global Tech:** paused H-1B offers since Oct 2025. **AMD, Palantir:** forum-only pause reports, unconfirmed.
- **Private stock** (Anthropic, OpenAI, Databricks, Figma and others) pays only on a sale or IPO. Compare offers on base plus a discounted stock value.
- **Level names differ:** Google SWE II = L3, so mid = L4. Amazon SDE II = L5. Microsoft SDE II = 61–62.
- **Transfers:** a change-of-employer inside the US never needed the $100K fee. Do not leave the US while it is pending.
