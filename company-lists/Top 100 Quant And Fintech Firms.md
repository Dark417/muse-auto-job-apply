## 1. How to read
- 100 firms with strong businesses that pay engineers well. Ranked by business strength + pay, not interview difficulty.
- **Firm -N** = priority. -0 apply now and chase a referral. -1 apply this week. -2 opportunistic.
- **Comp cell:** `$TC = $base + ($stock, $bonus)`. US dollars, thousands, per year, one midpoint each.
  - Two columns: **SWE2** (mid, 4–6 yrs) and **Sr SWE** (SWE3).
  - Stock = grant spread over 4 years. Funds and market makers pay cash, so it is $0.
  - `~` = my estimate. `~~` = very wide. No mark = anchored to levels.fyi or reports.
  - Sr SWE is rough: about SWE2 TC × 1.3–1.5.
- **Type:** MM market maker/prop. HF hedge fund. FT fintech. CR crypto. EX exchange/data/brokerage.
- **H-1B:** Filer = in labor-filing data. Verify = unconfirmed. Ask the recruiter about a transfer.

## 3. Watch-outs
- **Best 6–8 for you:** Two Sigma, Citadel, Millennium, HRT, Stripe, Ramp, Plaid, Robinhood. Platform/data/infra roles, not quant dev.
- **Texas hubs:** HRT, Optiver, Jump, Virtu, Akuna, CME (Austin). Two Sigma, Quantlab (Houston). Apex (Dallas). SoFi (Frisco).
- **Pod shops** (Millennium, Point72, Balyasny, ExodusPoint, Schonfeld): a closed pod can end your job. Risky on H-1B without an approved I-140.
- **Bonus is not guaranteed.** At funds and market makers it is 40–60% of TC. Compare base first.
- **Private fintech stock is paper** (Stripe, Ramp, Plaid, Brex, Kalshi). Discount it.
- **Interviews:** Jane Street, Optiver, IMC, SIG lean on probability and market-making games.
